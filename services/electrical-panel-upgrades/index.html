Panel and service upgrades in Keswick.

When a Keswick panel upgrade is meant to give a second living unit its own supply, the first question is how that unit is fed today. Earlier work with no records can make that hard to see.

A panel and an electrical service are different parts of the system

The panel distributes electricity to property circuits. The service is the incoming supply and associated equipment. Replacing a panel does not automatically increase the available supply. A Licensed Electrical Contractor should assess existing condition and demand, planned loads and the utility requirements before recommending equipment. Spare breaker spaces alone do not establish capacity.

Prepare the scope for your Keswick property

Sketch which rooms belong to each unit, and photograph the panel door, any labels on it and the meter position from the outside. Note any extra cables or equipment that came with the house and that you cannot explain, without touching them.

Ask whether the proposal separates the second unit's supply or only its circuits, who must be told about the change, and how the contractor will find out what the earlier installation connected together.

What should the assessment establish?

Before the owner approves a separate supply for a second living unit, or its timing, the scope shows how each unit in the Keswick home is fed today and whether any circuits cross between them. Earlier work that changed the arrangement is identified, and where it could not be confirmed, the scope says so plainly. A Licensed Electrical Contractor explains whether separate supply is a service matter, a panel matter or both, and whether the utility is involved. Circuits that move, who approves the change and what the ESA notification covers are listed. So is any assumption behind the price.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

What is the difference between a sub-panel and a separate supply?

A sub-panel is fed from the existing supply, while a separate supply is its own connection. Which one suits a second unit depends on the assessment, so ask the contractor to explain the reasoning and who must approve it.

How do I tell if earlier work already split the two units?

Labels may hint at it, but only an assessment by the contractor can confirm. Photograph labels as they are, note any circuit that seems to serve both units, and do not rely on a previous owner's memory alone.
